🖥️ Desktop & Tablet View 1920 × 500 px (or 1600 × 420 px)

Sleek ultra-widescreen ratio (~16:4 / ~4:1). Perfectly matched to the new compact desktop hero banner height (~370px). Keeps bottles and text crisp with zero vertical bloat.

{# Dimension 2: Mobile View #}
📱 Mobile Smartphone View 800 × 800 px (or 750 × 850 px)

Square (1:1) or vertical (~5:6) ratio tailored for smartphone viewports where hero height is ~370px.

• Why upload a mobile banner? Phones have narrow vertical viewports. Wide desktop banners get cropped on the left & right sides. An 800 × 800 px mobile image ensures your perfume bottles and models remain centered and razor-sharp.
• Auto-Fit Protection: If no separate mobile image is uploaded, our engine automatically center-crops the desktop image for phones.
